Material corrections are recorded here. Quietly fixing a typo is sensible;
quietly changing what a page claims is not.

## November 14, 2026

- **Maltese grant chronology.** The Heraldry page previously described the
  February 16, 2024 Gazette entry as publication of the grant. Notice No. 231
  announced the application and opened a 21-day period for representations.
  Letters Patent G0102 followed on May 1, 2024. The page now distinguishes the
  two documents and links to both.
- **Rubin de la Borbolla blazon.** The published blazon omitted the tincture of
  the three estoiles. It now specifies Or, matching the existing artwork;
  the armorial design has not changed.

## September 2, 2026

- **Emperor penguin conservation status.** Earlier copy said the emperor
  penguin was listed as Endangered on the IUCN Red List. That was incorrect.
  The page now describes observed breeding failures and links directly to the
  British Antarctic Survey without misstating the listing.
- **Fellowship scope.** Earlier pages described the Fellowship principally as
  recognition for polar researchers and communicators. The program is an annual
  $1,000 grant for graduate research in climate change and global conservation.
  The Fellowship and research-support pages now reflect the actual program.
- **Ocean acidification figure.** An animated homepage statistic claimed a 40%
  rise in ocean acidity. It was first replaced with an older NOAA estimate of
  about 30%. The site now uses NOAA's current synthesis: the ocean has become
  about 26% more acidic on average over the past 250 years, with the source and
  measurement context attached.
- **Territorial language.** Earlier copy called three Antarctic features the
  Count's “territories.” The site now states that the dignities correspond to
  real places but convey no ownership, jurisdiction, or sovereignty.
