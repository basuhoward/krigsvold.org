James P. Howard, II is the Count of Krigsvold. He uses the office as a durable
platform for climate education, research support, and public advocacy, with
Antarctica as its geographic and intellectual point of departure.

The distinction between title and work matters. The title is a dignity within
the Grand Duchy of Westarctica and confers no governmental or territorial
authority. The work conducted under it—including the annual Fellowship—is
public, practical, and open to scrutiny.

## Background

James is a data scientist and applied mathematician at the Johns Hopkins
University Applied Physics Laboratory. He holds a doctorate in Public Policy
and graduate degrees in Applied and Computational Mathematics, Environmental
Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Public Policy, among others.

He is a Chartered Engineer, Chartered Mathematician, and Chartered Scientist,
and a Fellow of the British Computer Society and the Institute of Mathematics
and its Applications. He teaches across mathematics, statistics, and public
affairs and serves as a Captain in the Maryland Defense Force.

That breadth shapes the work of Krigsvold. Climate change is simultaneously a
physical process, a problem of measurement, a matter of institutions, and a
public-policy choice. The site is written to keep those layers connected without
pretending they are interchangeable.

## Responsibilities of the office

The Count sets the editorial direction of this site, sponsors the Fellowship,
selects its recipient in partnership with Westarctica, and speaks publicly in
support of climate and conservation action. He is accountable for the claims
published here and for correcting them when they are wrong.

## Relationship to Westarctica

The Count holds his dignity within the Grand Duchy of Westarctica. Westarctica
is a micronational project and nonprofit organization; it is not a sovereign
state. Krigsvold's work is conducted within that context but published as the
independent work of this office.

For Westarctica's own activities, visit
[westarctica.info](https://www.westarctica.info),
[Encyclopedia Westarctica](https://www.westarctica.wiki), or
[Westarctica's nonprofit site](https://www.westarctica.org).
