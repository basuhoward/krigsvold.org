Like most websites, krigsvold.org and its hosting infrastructure may automatically process limited technical information when a page or other resource is requested. This may include:

* Your Internet Protocol address;
* The date and time of the request;
* The page or resource requested;
* The referring page, if supplied by your browser;
* Browser, device, and operating-system information; and
* Information needed to detect errors, abuse, or threats to the website.

This information may appear in ordinary server and security logs. It is used to deliver the website, maintain its reliability and security, diagnose technical problems, and prevent abuse. Where applicable, this processing is based on my legitimate interests in operating and protecting the website.

Server and security logs are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, diagnostic, and security purposes, subject to the practices of the website’s hosting and infrastructure providers.

I use this information to read and respond to your communication, maintain appropriate records, and take any action you request or authorize. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the purpose of your communication.

## Cookies

A cookie is a small piece of information stored by your browser. This website uses one necessary cookie to remember your privacy choice and, only with your consent, Google Analytics cookies.

### Consent cookie

The website stores a cookie named `cookieConsent` after you accept or reject analytics cookies. This cookie records your decision and the version of the cookie notice under which it was made.

The consent cookie:

* Is necessary to remember your choice;
* Is not used to track your activity;
* Is available only to this website;
* Is normally retained for 365 days; and
* May be replaced sooner if you change your choice or if this notice changes materially.

Because this cookie is used only to remember the privacy choice you requested, it is treated as necessary and does not require separate consent.

## Cookie Settings

Google Analytics is optional. It will not load, contact Google, or set analytics cookies unless you affirmatively select **Accept analytics**.

You may select **Reject analytics** without losing access to any part of this website. Ignoring or closing the request is not treated as consent.

You may review or change your choice at any time by selecting **Cookie Settings** in the footer of any page. If you withdraw consent, the website will disable future Analytics collection and attempt to remove the Google Analytics cookies associated with this website from your browser.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

If your browser sends a recognized Global Privacy Control signal, this website treats that signal as a rejection of optional analytics.

## Google Analytics

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the site. This helps identify useful and popular content, understand general traffic patterns, and improve the website.

Google Analytics may process information such as:

* Pages viewed;
* Approximate visit times and durations;
* General referral sources;
* General geographic information;
* Browser, device, and operating-system information; and
* Interactions with pages and site features.

Google Analytics may set cookies including:

| Cookie  | Purpose                                                            | Typical duration |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------: |
| `_ga`   | Distinguishes one browser from another for statistical measurement |  Up to two years |
| `_ga_*` | Maintains session information for a particular Analytics property  |  Up to two years |

The precise names and expiration periods may change as Google updates the service. These cookies are not set unless you accept analytics.

The website configures Google Analytics with advertising storage, advertising user data, advertising personalization, Google Signals, and advertising-personalization signals disabled. Google Analytics is not used to display targeted advertising on this website.

User-level and event-level Analytics data are retained in Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months. Aggregated statistical reports that no longer identify a particular browser or visit may be retained for a longer period.

Google may process Analytics information on servers located outside your country. Google is responsible for its own processing and transfer safeguards. Additional information is available in [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its explanation of [how Google uses information from sites and applications that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
