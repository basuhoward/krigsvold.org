Sheherazade is a doctoral researcher in Environmental Science, Policy, and
Management at the University of California, Berkeley. She received the first
Count of Krigsvold Fellowship in 2025.

## The research

Her work examines decentralized models of forest governance in Central
Sulawesi, Indonesia, focusing on Conservation Partnerships and Customary Forest
initiatives. It asks how Indigenous and local communities can exercise genuine
leadership in conservation—and how policy and scientific practice can recognize
the knowledge on which that stewardship depends.

Conservation succeeds or fails through institutions as well as ecosystems.
Rules about who may use land, whose evidence counts, and who shares the benefits
shape environmental outcomes. By studying these arrangements in practice, the
project connects biocultural stewardship to more equitable and effective forest
protection.

## Why the Fellowship supported it

The research treats local knowledge as part of rigorous conservation practice,
not as an ornamental consultation after decisions have been made. It addresses
climate, biodiversity, governance, and justice as connected problems.
