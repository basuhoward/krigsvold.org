Two Antarctic briefings have been revised, and the Fellowship page now includes
the published schedule for the next award. Together, these changes follow the
work from evidence and institutional decisions to practical research support.

## Marine protection requires an adopted decision

[Polar Ecosystems](/endangered-ecosystems/#ccamlr-2026) adds the reported outcome
of the 2026 CCAMLR negotiations. The section identifies its source and separates
Krigsvold's policy assessment from the account of the meeting.

## A satellite transition needs a usable record

[Research and Response](/research-response/#suomi-npp-2026) documents the end of
Suomi NPP data delivery and the designated successor services. The revision
examines continuity across platforms and links NOAA's confirmation.

## The next Fellowship cycle

The [2027 application schedule](/fellowship/#applications) is now
available on the Fellowship page, with a link to Westarctica's controlling
program information. Applications are not yet open.

The [Annual Record](/record/) records these updates without changing the
number of awards or formal positions. Separate heraldic corrections are
listed in the [Corrections record](/corrections/).
