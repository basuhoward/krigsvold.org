Three of Krigsvold's Antarctic briefings have been updated. The changes add
recent evidence without discarding the observations that came before it.

## Two ends of the sea-ice year

[Warming and Circulation](/warming-continents/#september-2026-maximum) now pairs
February's relatively near-average minimum with September's third-lowest
maximum. The two figures describe different parts of the seasonal cycle, not
competing verdicts on the same measurement. The new section links NASA's
[October 12 account](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/antarctic-sea-ice-grows-to-its-2026-maximum/)
and NSIDC's dated analysis of the early spring retreat.

## Different species, different pressures

[Polar Ecosystems](/endangered-ecosystems/#conservation-status-2026) incorporates
the [April IUCN assessments](https://www.bas.ac.uk/news/emperor-penguin-and-antarctic-fur-seal-now-endangered-due-to-climate-change-iucn-red-list/)
and the [October Zavodovski research](https://www.bas.ac.uk/news/penguin-population-collapse-on-volcanic-island/).
The revision distinguishes climate-related habitat and food changes from
disease, and an adopted conservation category from a proposed reassessment.
The island's contrasting penguin trajectories show why ecosystem change cannot
be reduced to one population moving in one direction.

## Measurements need more than instruments

[Research and Response](/research-response/#nsidc-analysis-continuity) adds the
NSIDC case: monthly sea-ice commentary resumed after a funding-related pause,
while maps and charts had continued. Its
[restart announcement](https://nsidc.org/sea-ice-today/analyses/nsidcs-analysis-site-sea-ice-today-returns)
and programme-status page identify what returned and what remained suspended.
The example makes a distinction central to Krigsvold's infrastructure position:
collecting data and making it useful require different, connected work.

The permanent briefings contain the supporting sources and limits. This note
records the October 13, 2026 update; it is not a live conditions report.
