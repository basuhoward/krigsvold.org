James Howard, Count of Krigsvold, and the Grand Duchy of Westarctica have
established the **Count of Krigsvold Fellowship**, an annual $1,000 grant for
graduate research in climate change and global conservation.

The Fellowship is open to master's and doctoral students worldwide. It is
designed to help emerging researchers investigate environmental problems whose
consequences cross national boundaries, from ecosystem conservation and climate
resilience to the governance of threatened landscapes.

## From title to practical support

Howard sponsors the Fellowship as a direct expression of the purpose behind the
Count of Krigsvold: using a symbolic Antarctic title to support serious work.
Westarctica administers the application and selection process through its
conservation program.

The inaugural application cycle closed on March 1, 2025. Sheherazade, a
doctoral researcher at the University of California, Berkeley, was subsequently
selected as the [first Count of Krigsvold Fellow](/fellows/sheherazade/).

## A continuing program

Current eligibility, deadlines, and application instructions are available on
the [Fellowship page](/fellowship/) and the official [Westarctica conservation
scholarship page](https://www.westarctica.org/conservation-scholarship). Each
award is also documented in the [Annual Record](/record/).
