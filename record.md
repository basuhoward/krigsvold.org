The annual record makes a small institution legible. It reports what Krigsvold
funded and published, where the work fell short, and what comes next.

| Measure | Record |

| Graduate research awards | 1 award of $1,000 |
| Fellow | [Ria Raut](/fellows/ria-raut/) |

The 2026 Fellowship application cycle opened in March. Ria Raut was announced
as the recipient in May. This site was subsequently rebuilt around a clearer
distinction between the symbolic title and the practical work conducted under
it.

## Cumulative record

Since 2025, the Fellowship has supported **two graduate researchers with $2,000
in awards**. Award terms and future application dates are maintained by
[Westarctica's official Fellowship page](https://www.westarctica.org/conservation-scholarship).

## Priorities for the next cycle

- publish each Fellow's year-end research update when available;
- add issue dates and primary sources to all public briefings;
- keep application dates and award information synchronized with Westarctica;
- expand the record beyond announcements to outcomes.
