The Count of Krigsvold

What this is

A symbolic dignity with a practical purpose

Krigsvold is a dignity within the Grand Duchy of Westarctica, a micronational project without sovereign recognition. The dignity is symbolic. The fellowship, educational work, research support, and public advocacy conducted under it are real.
